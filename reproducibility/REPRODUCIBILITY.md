## Execution

The notebooks contain the computational workflows used for model
development, inference, validation, analysis, metadata generation, and
visualization.

The reported experiments were verified by executing the corresponding
notebooks without runtime errors.

## Data and instrument materials

Access to original instrument materials and derived datasets is subject to
their respective provenance and licensing conditions.

## Reproducibility principle

The released artifacts are versioned together so that the code, manuscript,
figures, results, and model metadata associated with a given release can be
identified as a single reproducible research state.
